Peer-to-Peer Fundraising:
What the Market Ships, and What ideali.io Must Build

A review of 12 nonprofit SaaS platforms — what each one offers for peer-to-peer (P2P) fundraising, what it costs, what customers praise and complain about, and the exact feature scope ideali.io needs in order to compete.

1

The short version

Read this first. Peer-to-peer fundraising means the charity does not ask for money directly. Instead, supporters each get their own page and ask their own friends and family. The charity supplies the platform; the supporters supply the network. Every serious competitor now ships this. It is no longer a differentiator — it is the price of entry.

P2P is table stakes

Every credible competitor offers it, but they deliver it in three different ways — free and bundled, locked behind a paid tier, or built as a heavyweight enterprise product.

Two platforms stand apart: DonorSnap has no P2P at all, and Fundraise Up's P2P ("Fundraisers") is only in Early Access as of mid-2026.

The gap ideali.io should target

The mid-market. Build native individual and team pages, team captains, leaderboards, gamification and event tie-in.

Most important of all: automatic sync of fundraiser records AND donor records into the core CRM, with no separate reconciliation step. Across every review set, this CRM-native data flow is the single most valued and the single most complained-about dimension.

Who to benchmark against

  • Bonterra DonorDrive — enterprise depth, mobile app, gamification
  • Classy / GoFundMe Pro — team hierarchies, branding, event registration
  • Givebutter — frictionless free UX, auto-enabled P2P

Clearest 2026 innovation vectors: AI (Fundraise Up's AI Coach, Bloomerang AI) and native social sharing (the DonorDrive–Meta integration).

2

The three delivery models

Every platform in the set packages P2P in one of these ways. The packaging choice drives price, target customer, and how much of the product a buyer must purchase to get P2P at all.

Model A

Native + free (donor-tip funded)

Zeffy, Givebutter.

P2P included with individual and team pages, leaderboards and CRM — no platform fee. Revenue comes from an optional tip the donor adds at checkout.

Model B

Native but gated / add-on / sister-product

Donorbox (Pro tier), DonorPerfect ("Crowdfunding" module + partner integrations), Bloomerang Fundraising (P2P module inside the former Qgiv product; syncs to Bloomerang CRM), Keela (no native P2P — delivered through sibling product Raisely under Aplos), GiveSmart (included in a flat-fee contract), Funraise (Premium plan).

Model C

Enterprise P2P specialists

Bonterra DonorDrive, Classy / GoFundMe Pro, Funraise.

Built for large walk / run / ride programs. Fundraise Up is entering this space with an AI-first Early Access product.

Model D

No P2P

DonorSnap.

Competes on CRM depth and price instead. Useful to ideali.io as the "what happens when you don't build P2P" reference point.

3

CRM data flow — the most decision-critical dimension

Why this matters in plain terms. When a supporter raises money through their own page, two kinds of record are created: the fundraiser (the supporter running the page) and the donors (the friends who gave). If those records do not land in the charity's main donor database automatically, someone has to export spreadsheets and merge them by hand every campaign. That manual merge is the number one complaint in this market.

Truly native — one system

Donors and fundraisers land directly in the same platform's CRM.

Zeffy, Givebutter, GiveSmart, Funraise, Fundraise Up, DonorPerfect, Donorbox.

Native sync between sibling products

Bloomerang Fundraising to Bloomerang CRM — uses Bloomerang matching logic, creates soft credits for P2P donations, avoids duplicate constituents.

Keela ↔ Raisely ↔ Aplos.

API / integration to an external CRM

Classy / GoFundMe Pro — Salesforce, DonorPerfect.

Bonterra DonorDrive — connects to an organisation's existing stack.

Strategic read

ideali.io already owns the CRM. Shipping P2P that writes into it natively — matched, de-duplicated, soft-credited — turns the market's biggest complaint into ideali.io's headline feature. No competitor in the gated or enterprise camp does this cleanly for the mid-market.

Complaints: expensive, complex, innovation slowdown and support-rep churn after the GoFundMe merger, and auto-renewal surprises.

Described by a verified Capterra reviewer as "the best in-class software for peer to peer fundraising". Praise for responsiveness, the mobile app, and event / story features.

#1 on G2 across multiple nonprofit categories. Praised for free P2P, ease of use, and its weighted leaderboards.

Praised for personalised P2P pages, Facebook integration, and ease of use.

The Lauren McCluskey Foundation nearly tripled its donor count on its first Qgiv P2P campaign.

GiveSmart

Praised for its all-features-included flat fee and P2P ease of use.

Recurring complaint: cost.

Funraise

Praised for well-designed P2P and donation-form layouts.

Complaints: occasional bugs, and fee transparency as needs grow.

Platform-by-platform detail

The full picture on each of the 12, in the order the source spec presents them.

Zeffy Native, free

Best for: small and all-volunteer nonprofits

The only genuinely $0-fee P2P option — no platform fee, no transaction fee, no card fee. Funded by optional donor tips (up to 17%, default-on, which some report causes donor confusion).

Native P2P campaign type with individual and team pages, solo / team / captain toggles, a public leaderboard ranked by amount raised, donor recognition and anonymity options, and per-fundraiser page customisation: title, goal, story, profile photo, banner, colour, custom thank-you message.

Donors auto-flow into Zeffy's free CRM. Event tie-in exists — you can link a ticketing / registration form and require a registration fee (walk-a-thon) — but group registration is not supported through P2P.

Donorbox Pro tier

Best for: small–mid orgs already embedding Donorbox forms

P2P is native but gated to the Pro tier (~$139–150/mo). Platform fees: 2.95% on organisation P2P and 3.95% on supporter P2P at Standard; reduced to 1.75–2% at Pro / Premium.

Features: customisable individual pages, team fundraising, individual goals, donor wall, social sharing buttons, and 22+ payment methods via OmniGive.

Donations from all child campaigns flow to the parent org account. Native Donorbox CRM (add-on at Premium). Event ticketing is a separate module. G2 4.6 (1,378 reviews).

DonorPerfect Crowdfunding module

Best for: mid-size orgs prioritising donor management with light P2P

A CRM-first platform. P2P is delivered as a "Crowdfunding" capability on its online forms — supporters create personal pages straight from the donation form — plus partner integrations.

Gift and donor data sync seamlessly and automatically into the DonorPerfect CRM — its core strength.

P2P feature depth (team hierarchies, captains, gamification) is thin relative to specialists. Platform from $125+/mo with a 1.5% transaction fee.

DonorSnap No P2P

Best for: small–mid CRM-only

No P2P and no raffle tools — the vendor states this directly in its own comparison pages.

It is a full CRM: custom fields, segmentation, batch entry, pledge tracking, QuickBooks / Constant Contact integration, native event registration, and AuctionSnap. Pricing by contact count: $50/mo (up to 1,000) to $250/mo (30,000).

Relevance to ideali.io: a "what happens when you don't build P2P" reference. It competes on CRM depth and price, not fundraising breadth.

Keela Via Raisely

Best for: mid orgs wanting CRM + fundraising via the Aplos / Keela / Raisely stack

No native P2P. Now owned by Aplos, which also acquired Raisely. P2P, appeal pages and ticketing are delivered by Raisely and synced into Keela's CRM and Aplos accounting.

G2 lists a P2P feature reviewed by 40 users (social / shareable fundraising pages), but this reflects the combined offering, not Keela alone. Keela from ~$99/mo.

GiveSmart Included in all plans

Best for: mid–large orgs running galas, walks / runs / rides, tournaments

(Momentive Software.) P2P is included in every contract at no add-on cost — a deliberate anti-"nickel-and-dime" positioning.

Unlimited P2P campaigns, customisable team and individual pages, leaderboards, per-user text-to-donate, social sharing, an in-app message center, and community chat.

Over 41,000 P2P pages created on the platform. GiveSmart's own blog cites "$126 as the average donation to a GiveSmart-powered P2P campaign."

Pricing is a flat-fee subscription (third-party sources cite from ~$1,195/mo, or ~$3,000/yr entry) plus a 3.5% card fee (3.95% Amex). Native within the GiveSmart suite; P2P can tie to events with item / event management. G2 4.5 (157).

Bloomerang Fundraising (formerly Qgiv) Native module

Best for: mid orgs ($250k–$10M+) wanting P2P + CRM in one family

Qgiv was acquired by Bloomerang (Jan 2024) and rebranded. P2P is a native module within the fundraising product.

Rich P2P: milestone badges, competitive leaderboards, contests, team fundraising, team captains, branded event pages with custom themes, Facebook integration, and an online store with P2P registrations.

Data syncs to Bloomerang CRM using matching logic (no duplicate constituents), with soft credits for P2P donations.

Strong event tie-in: registration, walk-a-thons, and (July 2026) date-based / early-bird registration fees. This is the closest functional analog to what ideali.io would build.

Givebutter Native, free

Best for: small–mid orgs (typically <$5M) replacing 5–7 tools

Native and free — 0% platform fee with donor tips; up to 5% if tips disabled; Plus from $29/mo. P2P is automatically enabled on Page and Event campaigns.

Individual, team and multi-team fundraising; team captains; countdown timers; goal thermometers; a social-style supporter wall; automated fundraiser emails; social and email sharing.

Leaderboards weighted by both dollars raised and number of donors — a notable fairness mechanic: "A team member who raises $1,000 from 2 donors is ranked the same as someone who raises $200 from 10 donors."

Native free CRM. Event campaigns support "registration fundraising". G2 4.7 (1,984); Capterra 4.8. #1 on G2 across four nonprofit categories.

Classy / GoFundMe Pro Enterprise

Best for: large / enterprise multi-channel programs

Enterprise-grade. P2P is a core native pillar with the highest dedicated G2 P2P-feature score (9.2/10).

Supports "Classic peer-to-peer" and "Registration with fundraising" campaign types, team fundraising, admin-created and pre-built teams, team captains, leaderboards, deep branding (hex codes, custom domains / domain masking), and Classy Live for virtual and hybrid events with synced leaderboards.

Native Salesforce and DonorPerfect integrations. Pricing is non-public: ~$300+/mo, annual contracts (commonly $3,000+/yr), plus ~2.9% + $0.30 processing. G2 4.4 (146).

Complaints centre on cost, complexity, and post-GoFundMe-merger innovation slowdown and support churn.

Funraise Premium plan

Best for: mid / growing orgs wanting Classy-style capability without an enterprise contract

P2P is native but requires the Premium plan (from $99/mo flat; a free tier exists without P2P). Platform fee up to 5% (typically donor-covered) plus 60¢/transaction.

Features: branded, mobile-optimised campaign sites with video and images; supporter pages with progress bars and storytelling; Facebook Fundraiser sync; automated milestone and stalled-progress emails; "Register to Fundraise" for endurance events; and "Commitments" — the fundraiser's card is charged the shortfall if they do not hit a committed amount, useful for service trips.

Vendor-reported: "Fundraisers linked with Facebook Fundraisers raise 83% more," and "P2P fundraisers on Funraise raise $1,220, 2x the amount of top P2P programs." Native CRM.

Fundraise Up Early Access

Best for: mid–large digital-first orgs prioritising conversion and AI

P2P ("Fundraisers") is in Early Access, launched 29 July 2026 — not generally available.

Differentiators: runs natively on the nonprofit's own website (no separate microsite); an AI Coach — "Every fundraiser gets an AI Coach that drafts their story, suggests next steps when momentum stalls, and sends milestone nudges" — plus automated milestone emails at 25% / 50% / 100% of goal; AI page moderation; the same conversion-optimised checkout used across the platform (cited as an average 30% conversion lift); a self-service Donor Portal; and unified donor records / CRM sync ("nothing to reconcile").

Pricing: flat 4% transaction fee (5% crypto), no separate P2P fee disclosed; Early Access carries no fee or contract.

Notable gaps: leaderboards are not confirmed in current materials; company matching is a separate feature not confirmed as integrated into P2P; Events (walk / run / ride) are on the roadmap ("next"), not released. G2 4.8 (~86 reviews); Capterra ~92 reviews.

Bonterra DonorDrive Enterprise specialist

Best for: large / enterprise orgs running major walk / run / ride and DIY programs

The category's enterprise P2P specialist — 25+ years; acquired by Bonterra Sept 2024.

Deep native P2P: personal, team and event fundraising pages; team captains; gamification incentives; activity tracking (fitness / endurance); livestreaming; a native mobile app; and a clicks-not-code event builder (vendor: 6x faster setup).

Per a Bonterra / Business Wire release (March 2022), DonorDrive Charity Fundraising app users saw "a 195% increase in the average amount raised per fundraiser, a 240% increase in the average engagement rate among app users, and a 56% increase in the average adoption rate" — vendor-reported.

Award-winning CRM integration connects to an org's existing stack. The July 2025 native Meta integration lets pages post directly into Instagram / Facebook Feeds, Stories and Profiles with donate buttons.

Pricing is non-public: a "lite" version ships with Bonterra Fundraising Essentials, a fully featured version with Pro / Enterprise. G2 4.7 (265); Capterra ~4.2–4.7 (36 reviews). Complaints: reporting gaps / clunkiness and a post-acquisition roadmap slowdown with no AI upgrades yet.

What ideali.io must build

The recommendations from the spec, restated as decisions and scope. Each carries an identifier so it can be tracked into the backlog.

R1 · POSITIONING

Build, don't merely integrate — but scope to the mid-market first

The mid-market ($250k–$10M) is where the strongest native competitors cluster — Bloomerang Fundraising, Funraise, GiveSmart — and where CRM-native P2P data flow is both most valued and most frequently criticised.

Match this tier before attempting DonorDrive / Classy enterprise depth.

R2 · DIFFERENTIATOR

Make CRM-native data flow the flagship differentiator

The consistent complaint across the market is reconciliation friction. Ship P2P so that both fundraiser records and their donors land automatically in ideali.io's core CRM with:

  • matching logic — no duplicates
  • soft-credit attribution to fundraisers
  • unified engagement scoring

This is the exact model Bloomerang / Qgiv and Fundraise Up tout. Threshold to prioritise: if user research shows >30% of prospects currently run P2P on a separate tool from their CRM, this is the wedge.

R3 · V1 FEATURE FLOOR

Ship the proven feature floor in v1

These appear in every competitive native offering. Their absence is disqualifying.

R4 · 2026 VECTORS

Differentiate on two 2026 vectors

(a) AI assistance. An "AI Coach" that drafts fundraiser stories and sends nudges. This is Fundraise Up's launch feature; DonorDrive notably has none yet — a window.

(b) Native social sharing with in-feed donate buttons — the DonorDrive–Meta pattern. These are where incumbents are either just arriving or lagging.

R5 · EVENTS

Require event tie-in in v1.5, not v3

Every serious P2P competitor links P2P to ticketed / registered events (walk-a-thons, galas) — Zeffy, Givebutter, Bloomerang, Classy, Funraise, GiveSmart and DonorDrive all do.

Expected standard: a "registration with fundraising" campaign type plus early-bird / date-based fees (Bloomerang, July 2026).

R6 · PRICING

Pricing model

Avoid gating P2P behind a punitive tier if targeting small / mid orgs. GiveSmart's "all features included" and Givebutter / Zeffy's free models are winning goodwill, while Donorbox's per-feature platform fees and Classy's enterprise cost are top complaints.

A modest platform fee (1.5–2%) or a flat subscription with P2P included will out-position add-on gating.

Benchmark: if CAC research shows small orgs churning to Zeffy / Givebutter over fees, include P2P in the base tier.

What would change these recommendations

If ideali.io's user base skews enterprise (>$10M, endurance-event-heavy), reprioritise toward DonorDrive-class depth — native mobile app, livestreaming, activity tracking, clicks-not-code event builder — and accept a longer build.

If the base is overwhelmingly small / volunteer-run, a lighter Zeffy / Givebutter-style free-P2P wedge beats feature depth.

Caveats — read before quoting any figure

Every number in this document carries a confidence level. These are the limits of the underlying research.

Classy / GoFundMe Pro, Bonterra DonorDrive and GiveSmart do not publish standard pricing. Figures cited — for example Classy ~$300+/mo, GiveSmart ~$1,195/mo or ~$3,000/yr — come from third-party aggregators (aitoolsbakery, SelectHub, Software Finder) and should be treated as estimates, not vendor quotes. Confirm via vendor sales.

"154% increase in funds per campaign" (DonorDrive–Meta), "83% more" and "$1,220 / 2x" (Funraise Facebook sync), "6x faster setup", and the app-driven "195% / 240% / 56%" figures (DonorDrive) are vendor-reported and unverified by independent audit. Fundraise Up's "30% conversion lift" and "nearly doubled" early-access figures are likewise self-reported.

Its feature set will change. Leaderboards and P2P-integrated company matching are currently unconfirmed, and Events are unreleased.

Only Classy's 9.2/10 P2P score is a dedicated P2P-feature rating on G2. Most other star ratings — Givebutter 4.7, DonorDrive 4.7 — are whole-product. G2 and Capterra were consolidated under common ownership in early 2026, and G2 permits incentivised reviews. Cross-check with TrustRadius where possible.

Keela's P2P is effectively Raisely's, and DonorPerfect's P2P is a lighter "crowdfunding" form feature. Neither is a full standalone P2P module comparable to DonorDrive or Classy.

For example, DonorDrive Capterra shows both ~36 and 265 depending on domain and date. Treat all counts as approximate.

Glossary

P2P (peer-to-peer)
Supporters fundraise on the charity's behalf from their own personal pages, instead of the charity asking donors directly.
Fundraiser
The supporter running a personal page. Not the donor, and not the charity.
Team captain
A fundraiser with permission to manage a team page and the members under it.
Soft credit
A CRM record that credits a fundraiser for a gift someone else actually paid, so their influence is visible without double-counting the money.
Matching logic
Rules that recognise an incoming person as an existing constituent, so the CRM does not create a duplicate record.
Goal thermometer
The progress bar on a page showing how close a fundraiser, team or campaign is to its target.
Weighted leaderboard
A ranking that counts both money raised and number of donors, so a fundraiser with many small gifts is not beaten purely by one large gift.
Zero-dollar fundraiser
A supporter who signs up, gets a page, and never raises a single gift. The main efficiency problem in P2P programs.
Stalled-progress email
An automatic nudge sent when a fundraiser's page stops receiving gifts.
Registration with fundraising
A campaign type where signing up for an event (a run, a walk) also creates the participant's fundraising page.
Commitments
Funraise's mechanic where a fundraiser's own card is charged the shortfall if they miss an amount they committed to raise.
Donor tip
An optional extra the donor adds at checkout to fund the platform, used instead of charging the charity a fee.
CAC
Customer acquisition cost — what it costs to win one paying customer.

The one-sentence takeaway

P2P itself will not win ideali.io a customer — everyone has it. What wins is P2P whose fundraiser and donor records land in ideali.io's own CRM automatically, matched and soft-credited, with nothing to reconcile — shipped with the full v1 feature floor, event tie-in by v1.5, and priced so a mid-market org is not made to pay extra for it.
